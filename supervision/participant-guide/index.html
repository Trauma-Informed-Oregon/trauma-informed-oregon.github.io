A few things to know while you are here

ChoiceSharing is optional. You can pass, observe, or take a break.
ConfidentialityProtect names, identifying details, and information that is not yours to share.
Low-activation practiceUse hypothetical examples or real situations around a 2 or 3 on a 10-point activation scale.
Power and contextNotice how identity, role, history, culture, and power shape what is happening.
