Welcome.

This four-part series is a place to strengthen how you supervise, especially when identity, power, stress, conflict, and organizational pressure are in the room. You do not need to prepare a personal story. Bring your questions, your experience, and your willingness to practice.

Series at a glance

Four sessions. One connected practice.

Each session adds another layer to the work of supervision. You do not need to master any of it in one sitting.

Module 1

Trauma, Healing & Identity in Supervision

Identity, power, context, equity, and parallel process.

Module 2

Cultivating a Resilient Supervisor & Supervisee Relationship

Four strategies for relationship, dialogue, trust, and follow-through.

Module 3

Proactive Approaches to Navigating Conflict & Tension

Fear, accountability, conflict mapping, and compassionate curiosity.

Module 4

Developing Self-Awareness & Intentional Leadership Style

Integration, advocacy, and the leadership choices you want to practice.

Working agreements

Make room for choice, context, and different ways of participating.

Choice

Sharing is optional. You may pass, observe, use the chat, step away, or take a break.

Confidentiality

Stories stay, lessons leave. Protect identifying details about supervisees, colleagues, and people served.

Low-activation practice

When using real situations, choose something around a 2 or 3 on a 10-point activation scale. Hypothetical examples are welcome.

Power and context

Notice how identity, role, culture, history, and organizational power shape what is happening and what choices are available.
