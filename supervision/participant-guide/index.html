Series at a glance

Four sessions that build on each other.

You do not have to master any of this in one sitting. Each module adds another way to notice what is happening and decide what to do next.

For the learning space

A few things to keep in mind.

Keep practice low-activation

Use examples that are safe enough to think about clearly. You can stay with a hypothetical situation if that feels more useful.

Notice power and context

Role, identity, workload, policy, and history shape what is possible. The same moment does not carry the same risk for everyone.

Take what is useful

You are not expected to use every tool the same way. Use what helps you prepare, reflect, and lead supervision with more intention.
