How the materials work

One series. Different tools for different moments.

The participant guide gives you the through-line. Each module has a practice kit that goes deeper into that session. Module 2 also includes a Strategy Lab for practicing supervisory judgment.

1
Use this guide across the series

Keep the series map, working agreements, and module links in one place.

2
Use the module kits after each session

Each kit gives you the concepts, questions, and working tools most likely to matter on the job.

3
Practice between sessions

Use the Strategy Lab, conflict map, advocacy planner, or your own low-activation example when you want rehearsal rather than more reading.

Working agreements

Use the space in ways that support your learning.

Choice

Sharing is optional. You may pass, observe, or take a break.

Confidentiality

Stories stay, lessons leave. Protect identifying details and confidentiality.

Low-activation practice

Use hypothetical examples or real examples around a 2 or 3 on a 10-point activation scale.

Power and context

Make room for multiple truths and norms. Notice how identity, role, history, and power shape what is happening.
