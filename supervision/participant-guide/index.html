Trauma Informed Supervision

Four sessions for supervisors and people who lead others. We’ll work with identity and power, the supervisor and supervisee relationship, conflict and tension, and intentional leadership.

You do not need to prepare a personal story for this training. Bring what is useful from your own experience, use hypothetical examples when that works better, and return to these tools when you want support applying the learning.

For the learning space

A few things to keep in mind.

Choice

Sharing is optional. You may pass, observe, or take a break.

Confidentiality

Protect names and identifying details when you use examples.

Keep practice low-activation

Use a hypothetical example or a real example around a 2 or 3 on a 10-point activation scale.

Notice power and context

Identity, role, history, workplace conditions, and power shape supervision.
