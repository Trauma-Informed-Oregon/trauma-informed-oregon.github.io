Breakout Sessions: Questions & Activities

Keep this page open during breakout groups. Find your module, then open the numbered breakout your facilitator announces. You can participate, listen, or use a hypothetical or de-identified example.

Underlined terms have short explanations: hover, tap, or focus with your keyboard.

Module 1

Who We Are Together

Select the breakout your facilitator announces. Each section has its own questions.

Breakout 1Trauma and Trauma-Informed Care⌄
  1. How would you describe trauma to a colleague?
  2. How would you describe trauma-informed care to a new staff member?
  3. What rises to the top from your varying definitions?
Breakout 2How We Do the Work⌄
  1. What does “how we do the work is the work” mean to you?
  2. How does it show up, or how might it show up, in the processes you lead?
  3. Gather key highlights to share with the larger group.

Module 2

Cultivating a Resilient Supervisor and Supervisee Relationship

Select the breakout your facilitator announces. Each section has its own questions.

Breakout 1Strengths in Supervision⌄
  1. Where do you already feel confident in equity-centeredAttending to how identity, power, access, and unequal conditions shape people's experiences and choices., trauma-informed supervision?
  2. How did you get there?
  3. If you aren’t sure yet, where would you like to feel more confident?
Breakout 2Strategies for Relationships⌄
  1. What strategies do you already know, or can imagine, for equity-centeredAttending to how identity, power, access, and unequal conditions shape people's experiences and choices., trauma-informed supervisor/supervisee relationships?
Breakout 3Both/And Practice⌄
  1. Consider a hypothetical supervisory challenge.
  2. Describe how a rigid either/or response might sound.
  3. Practice one of the both/and scripts from the session or create your own.
Breakout 4Open Dialogue Practice⌄
  1. Consider a situation in which a supervisor feels pressured or stressed to respond.
  2. Practice an open-dialogue script from the session, or create your own.

Module 3

Proactive Approaches to Navigating Conflict and Tension

Select the breakout your facilitator announces. Each section has its own questions.

Breakout 1Celebrating Success⌄
  1. Share your name, role, and agency. Pronouns are optional.
  2. In one sentence, describe a success or breakthrough in your relationship with a supervisee. Small successes count.
  3. Choose one word that sums up the success to share with the larger group.
Breakout 2Accountability⌄
  1. When you hear the word accountability, what immediate thoughts come to mind?
  2. How does the word resonate in your body (somaticallyNotice what happens in your body, such as tension, warmth, a tightened jaw, or ease. No personal disclosure is required.)?
Breakout 3Fear in Supervision⌄
  1. What are some strategies to address common fear and trauma that supervisors experience?
Breakout 4Generative Conflict⌄
  1. Think of a time when you experienced conflict as generativeLeading to something useful, such as better understanding, a change in practice, or a stronger relationship..
  2. What was the generativeLeading to something useful, such as better understanding, a change in practice, or a stronger relationship. outcome?

Module 4

Developing Self-Awareness and Intentional Leadership Style

Select the breakout your facilitator announces. Each section has its own questions.

Breakout 1Advocacy for Supervisees⌄
  1. In advocating for your supervisees, what has been successful?
  2. Where have you experienced personal or organizational roadblocks?
  3. Prepare to share highlights with the larger group.
Breakout 2Practice Scenarios⌄
  1. Use the scenario (1–10) assigned by your facilitator.
  2. Describe the scenario and identify action items for a supervisor.
  3. Consider systems and institutions, culture and society, community, and family; parallel processPatterns in one relationship showing up in another, such as the ways supervision affects staff interactions with clients.; conflict; fears; and advocacy needs.
