Steps to advocacy

A framework for deciding what to do next.

The current Module 4 materials describe these as connected steps. They are not always linear.

1
Listen

Understand before acting. Hear what the supervisee is actually saying.

2
Ask

Clarify what is needed and whether they want you to advocate, support them to advocate, or take another role.

3
Engage

Take action with the relevant person, system, policy, or decision-maker.

4
Develop

Build the strategy. Identify allies, timing, process, leverage, and knowledge you may need.

5
Reflect

After action, assess what worked, what did not, and what you learned.

6
Acknowledge

Name reality, impact, limitations, and what still needs attention.

Use it in real supervision

Turn reflection into a next move.

1
Listen and ask

Get clear about what the supervisee wants, what they do not want, and what role they want you to play.

2
Locate your role

Name what is within your authority, what requires another decision-maker, and where advocacy may be useful.

3
Act with them

Choose the next step, who needs to be involved, what needs to be communicated, and what follow-through belongs to you.

4
Reflect and acknowledge

Return to what happened, what changed, what did not, and what still needs attention.
